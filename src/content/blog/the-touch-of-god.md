![Creation of Adam](@assets/creation-of-adam.jpg)

It's the middle of 2026 and the world's most powerful AI model dropped. Suddenly, everybody is creating indie games with full-on 3D assets and animations and particles and everything that would have otherwise taken a whole team of designers and months of work.

Even I was able to get my hands on this new, powerful AI and build something [magical](https://quillium.bryanhu.com/?hero=3d-variant-2) myself.

Of course I'm talking about Claude Fable 5, the almighty Mythos rendered lobotomized enough for the public.

It's quite cool, funny, and sad all at the same time about the release and closing of this Pandora's box. A model where literally anything you can think of is probably possible. A model that will [create its own screenshotting/browser harness system](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/11/fable-is-relentlessly-proactive/) just to finish accomplishing a task. You could one-shot [entire](https://x.com/deedydas/status/2065456678154428809), [detailed](https://x.com/ChrissGPT/status/2065277854615572901) [3D](https://x.com/AlfinCodes/status/2065412299289153776) scenes with it (and clone a lot of other [games](https://x.com/tyrtyre201/status/2065469711190536464) that I will not be linking X posts lol).

Alas they giveth and taketh away. All good things must come to an end.

...or will it?
